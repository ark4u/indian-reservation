# Indian Reservation System Research & Analysis

This repository is dedicated to data analysis, research, and the examination of diverse perspectives regarding the reservation system in India. 

## Objectives
- **Constitutional & Legal Analysis:** Examining statutory frameworks, amendments, and Supreme Court jurisprudence.
- **Socio-Economic Data & Outcomes:** Analyzing statistical data, quota structures, and impact assessments.
- **Diverse Perspectives:** Compiling arguments across social justice, equity, administrative efficiency, meritocracy, and public policy debates.
- **Media & Discourse:** Reviewing expert discussions, panel debates, and public discourse.
